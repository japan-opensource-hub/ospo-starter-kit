Open source license compliance means maintaining organizational visibility over "what OSS is used, where, under what license terms, and how," ensuring continuous adherence to those licensing conditions across the entire software development lifecycle.

In open source software utilization, compliance represents a critical operational requirement. That open source permits free usage does not imply the absence of legal terms or obligations. Robust compliance management prevents licensing non-compliance and intellectual property issues, enabling sustainable open source adoption.

Open source compliance at [Company Name] covers the following essential domains:

## Fundamentals of License Management

Every open source software component carries a license; understanding and adhering to its terms represents the foundation of all open source activities. Core licensing concepts, enterprise considerations, and compliance workflows are detailed in **[What Is a License?](../compliance/what-is-a-license.md)**.

## License Classification and Selection Guidelines

Numerous open source licenses exist, each specifying distinct rights grants and obligations. Categorizations of license conditions and enterprise adoption guidelines are covered in **[Types of Licenses](../compliance/license-types.md)**.

## Managing Licenses with High Compliance Requirements

Licenses with source code disclosure obligations (e.g., GPL, AGPL, LGPL) carry extensive verification requirements based on distribution models. Specific characteristics and management workflows are explained in **[Licenses with Source Code Disclosure Obligations](../compliance/source-disclosure-licenses.md)**.

## Cases Requiring Commercial Licensing

Certain open source software requires commercial license purchases under specific usage conditions. Dual-licensing models, enterprise add-ons, and usage-scale triggers are detailed in **[OSS with Commercial License Options](../compliance/oss-with-commercial-license-options.md)**.

Managing these domains holistically minimizes legal exposure while maximizing open source value. Refer to corresponding sections for details, and contact the OSPO with questions.
