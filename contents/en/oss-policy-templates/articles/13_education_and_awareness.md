# Article 13. Education and Awareness

## Sample Article

Article 13. Education and Awareness

1. Employees using OSS shall complete relevant training covering basic OSS knowledge, license compliance, information security, and this Policy.

## Commentary

OSS violations stem primarily from lack of awareness; education represents the most effective preventive control. Given the diversity of open source licenses, baseline literacy is required for operational compliance. Because required knowledge varies by role (vulnerability response, distribution obligations, IP ownership), role-tailored training programs yield high efficacy. Policies fail unless communicated; continuous awareness raising builds organizational OSS literacy, enabling speed alongside governance.

## Customization Perspectives and Operational Notes

Design separate tracks for general employee foundational training and developer practical training.

- Use e-learning as a baseline while providing elective advanced sessions; partnering with external trainers or OSS communities is effective.
- Tailor role-based curricula: practical compliance and tool usage for engineers; risk management and decision-making for managers; legal interpretation of licenses for Legal; OSS disclosure and SBOM requests for Procurement/Sourcing.
- Enhance retention by teaching through concrete failure examples and DOs/DON'Ts case studies.
- Link attendance rates with Article 12 record-keeping to drive continuous program improvement.
