# Article 0. Preamble

## Sample Article

Article 0. Preamble

[Company Name] Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "the Company") establishes this Policy as a basic guideline concerning the use of Open Source Software (hereinafter referred to as "OSS") [and contributions to the OSS community].
OSS is an essential resource supporting software development productivity and technological innovation. The Company aims to enhance technical capabilities and create sustainable value through the utilization of OSS [and contributions to the community].
On the other hand, using OSS requires responsible handling, including license compliance, protection of intellectual property rights, and ensuring security and quality.
This Policy sets forth the fundamental principles and internal rules regarding OSS, establishing an environment where all Company stakeholders can utilize OSS safely and appropriately, thereby achieving appropriate and responsible conduct.

## Commentary

OSS forms the foundation of modern software development and business, and its utilization brings opportunities for efficiency, standardization, and innovation to enterprises. OSS is developed, improved, and maintained by open communities, and how an organization engages with these communities significantly impacts the success of its OSS utilization. This Preamble functions not merely as a compliance declaration, but as an entry point demonstrating strategic managerial intent, enabling consistent explanations internally and externally while suppressing variations in judgment across departments. By explicitly defining organizational OSS stages—"Use-centric, Community Contribution, and OSS Release"—it encourages moving beyond purely consumption-oriented activities and establishes an anchor for long-term decision-making.

## Customization Perspectives and Operational Notes

Customizing the preamble must account for both the company's OSS stage (Use-centric, Community Contribution, OSS Release) and its business domain.

- Highlighting the specific importance of OSS to your company—such as embedded software for manufacturing or cloud infrastructure for SaaS—will increase internal buy-in.
- Focus on 1–2 reasons "why you use OSS" (speed, quality, standardization, etc.), and add a sentence outlining your perspective on responsibility according to your business model (SI, SaaS, products, etc.).
- Adjust the phrasing strength for community contributions according to your OSS stage. If in early stages, keep it at "aiming to contribute"; companies with established track records should add specific contribution guidelines.
- Describing a development roadmap of "Use-centric -> Community Contribution -> OSS Release" clarifies both current status and future vision.
- Minimize technical jargon in this Policy, guide users to the OSS Operational Guidelines for details, and reference contact points (OSPO equivalent) and core postures (integrity, transparency, etc.) to foster actionable compliance.
