# Article 14. Relation to Employment Regulations

## Sample Article

Article 14. Relation to Employment Regulations

1. The positioning of OSS activities, working hours, performance evaluations, and side activities (moonlighting) shall comply with Employment Regulations and HR policies. The OSPO shall consult with HR and Legal to establish necessary interpretations or supplementary rules.

## Commentary

Ambiguity over whether OSS activity constitutes official duties or personal pursuit leads to labor, evaluation, and side-employment disputes. Community activities include tasks not directly tied to immediate assignments, creating working-hour accounting challenges. Contradictions with Employment Regulations create confusion and stall operations, making prior alignment with HR and Legal vital. Without alignment with appraisal systems, OSS activities deteriorate into formality; fostering a safe environment for open source engagement strengthens talent competitiveness.

## Customization Perspectives and Operational Notes

Initiate discussions with HR and Legal early to identify and resolve conflicts with primary policies (Employment Regulations, Side Employment Policy, Performance Review System).

- Define the scope of activities recognized as official business and align them with working hours. If side-employment policies exist, explicitly outline relations to personal OSS activities (disclosure requirements, permission criteria).
- Recognizing that total initial alignment is difficult, adopt an iterative approach to refine rules during actual operations.
