# Article 15. Revision and Enforcement

## Sample Article

Article 15. Revision and Enforcement

1. The enactment and revision of this Policy shall be drafted by the OSPO and executed through the Company's prescribed approval procedures.
2. This Policy shall take effect on the enforcement date specified by the Company.

## Commentary

Technology, legal frameworks, and community norms surrounding OSS evolve rapidly, necessitating periodic policy reviews. Explicitly defining a revision process where the OSPO drafts proposals for executive approval balances domain expertise with formal organizational authority. Specifying the enforcement date establishes a clear legal start point, while defining non-retroactivity rules manages impact on legacy software projects.

## Customization Perspectives and Operational Notes

- Specify review frequency (annual review recommended) and triggering events (legislative changes, major incidents, re-organizations) in the OSS Operational Guidelines. Define revision log and announcement protocols.
- Conduct an inventory of existing OSS usage prior to enforcement to identify non-compliant components, establishing a realistic remediation schedule that avoids overwhelming development teams.
- Clarify criteria for grandfathering legacy projects while leaving room to update legal interpretations as open source standards evolve.
