# Article 1. Purpose

## Sample Article

Article 1. Purpose

1. The purpose of this Policy is to establish fundamental matters concerning the use and management of OSS [as well as contributions to the OSS community] within the Company, thereby achieving the following objectives:
  a. Complying with OSS licenses
  b. Appropriately protecting patent rights, copyrights, and other intellectual property rights, while complying with relevant laws, regulations, and contractual obligations
  c. Appropriately protecting confidential information, personal data, and other critical information, maintaining the Company's credibility and brand value
  d. Enhancing business value and strengthening technological foundations through appropriate OSS utilization
  [e. Promoting contribution to and collaboration with the OSS community]
  f. Ensuring information security and software quality

## Commentary

This Article clarifies the overall goals of the Policy and serves as an interpretative foundation for each subsequent article. Non-compliance with licenses directly leads to litigation risks and product shipment halts. Furthermore, confidential information and personal data must be prevented from leaking through OSS activities. Aligning strategic OSS usage with business value positions OSS not merely as a risk avoidance mechanism, but as a driver of business contribution. It also provides the justification for establishing an OSPO and serves to publicly declare a commitment to continuous community engagement.

## Customization Perspectives and Operational Notes

Adjust the order and phrasing of objectives to align with your strategic priorities, as this article establishes the overall priority ranking for the Policy.

- Reflect your company's priorities—for example, place item (a) first if emphasizing compliance, or item (d) higher if driving innovation.
- Add tailored objectives such as "customer accountability" or "continuous operation and vulnerability response." Explaining concrete examples of "enhancing business value" (e.g., reducing development costs, accelerating time-to-market) in supplementary notes will deepen field comprehension.
- Even if community contributions or OSS releases are not planned immediately, keep these objectives as future options and restrict them operationally instead.
- Do not mix objectives (Why) with procedures (How); delegate procedural details to the OSS Operational Guidelines.
