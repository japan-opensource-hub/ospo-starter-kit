# Article 12. Records and Reporting

## Sample Article

Article 12. Records and Reporting

1. The OSPO shall create and retain records concerning the following matters for an appropriate period:
  a. Inventory of OSS used [and released] by the Company, licenses, [release status], and major history
  b. Key OSS usage status and license evaluation results
  [c. Key agreements concerning CLA executions, copyrights, and patents]
  d. Summary of license violations, incidents, and corrective measures
2. The OSPO shall report on Company OSS activities and risk posture to executive management at least once a year.

## Commentary

Without tracking utilized components, maintaining license compliance or vulnerability management is impossible; accurate record-keeping is essential. Visualizing OSS inventory establishes accountability for customer audits and due diligence. Retaining records of executed CLAs and strategic decisions tracks rights ownership, proving indispensable in legal disputes. Incident and remediation logs drive recurrence prevention and internal knowledge building, while annual executive reporting enables leadership to assess OSS health and make informed risk-investment decisions.

## Customization Perspectives and Operational Notes

Adopt a phased expansion starting with critical products before scaling company-wide. Begin with a minimal record-keeping dataset (OSS name, version, license, distribution flag).

- Minimize manual data entry by leveraging repository management tools (GitHub, etc.) and automated SBOM integration to sustain compliance operations.
- Centralize signed documents (CLAs) in contract management systems in coordination with Legal record-keeping rules.
- Maintain incident record templates covering factual timelines, root cause analysis, corrective actions, and prevention plans as standard fields.
- Define retention periods, access controls, and maintenance ownership to avoid reliance on specific individuals.
