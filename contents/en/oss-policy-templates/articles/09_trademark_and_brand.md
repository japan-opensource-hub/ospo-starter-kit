# Article 9. Handling of Trademarks and Branding

## Sample Article

Article 9. Handling of Trademarks and Branding

1. When displaying the Company name, logo, or other trademarks (hereinafter referred to as "Company Trademarks") in OSS activities, Employees shall comply with separately established Trademark Usage Policies.

## Commentary

Misuse of company names or logos creates false impressions of official endorsement, impacting corporate trust. Because OSS activities are public, trademark management for internal OSS influences community governance and brand integrity. Trademark governance involves Legal, PR, and IP teams alongside Engineering, making explicit inclusion in policy essential.

## Customization Perspectives and Operational Notes

If existing trademark policies do not cover open source use cases, add supplementary rules. Organize reference links to prevent duplicate rules.

- Establish trademark registration policies for Company-released OSS (criteria and decision-makers); standard practice limits costly trademark registration to flagship projects.
- Clarify guidelines for GitHub organization names, repository naming, and logo placement in the OSS Operational Guidelines.
- For third-party OSS logos/names, enforce compliance with "refer to each project's trademark guidelines" and maintain a directory of major project guidelines on an internal wiki.
- Align trademark corrective workflows (takedown requests, branding fixes) with Article 10 incident procedures.
