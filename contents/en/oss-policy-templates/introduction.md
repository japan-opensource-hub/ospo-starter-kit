# Introduction ─ How to Use This Document

## About This Document

This document is a template containing sample policy clauses, background rationales, and customization guidelines to assist in formulating an internal company-wide OSS Policy.
This document serves as a starting draft; customize and adapt it to align with your organization's structure, business domain, and existing regulations prior to formal adoption.

## Structure of This Document

Each article consists of the following three sections:

- **Sample Article**: Draft text of the article. Structured hierarchically: Article -> Paragraph -> Sub-item (a, b, c...). Use this wording as a baseline for your customized policy. Note: If your policy focuses solely on OSS usage (excluding contributions/releases), you can omit bracketed text marked with `【 】`.
- **Commentary**: Explains the purpose, background, and risk management rationale behind the article. Use this section when presenting justification to executive management or key stakeholders.
- **Customization Perspectives and Operational Notes**: Provides actionable insights for tailoring the policy to your company's scale, business model, and existing rules. Contains tips on items to add, remove, or consolidate.

## How to Apply Sample Articles

- **Step 1**: Read the "Sample Article" for each article and compare it against your company's current OSS practices and strategic goals.
- **Step 2**: Refer to "Customization Perspectives and Operational Notes" to delete unnecessary paragraphs/items or adjust phrasing.
- **Step 3**: Replace placeholders like "[Company Name] Co., Ltd." or "separately specified" with your company's actual information.
- **Step 4**: Verify alignment with existing internal regulations (Employment Regulations, Information Security Policy, Work-for-Hire Rules) in consultation with Legal and HR.
- **Step 5**: Replace references to "OSPO" in this Policy with your actual designated governing body (e.g., OSS Committee, Legal Dept, IT Dept).
